NOVAPRIME AI · ARCHITECTURE-DATABASE COMPILER

FPGA Compiler

Building the architecture-database compiler that turns FPGA resource definitions into implementation-ready device models.

Click to explore
+

At NovaPrime AI, I build an FPGA architecture-database compiler. The architecture database captures BELs, sites, tiles, wires, PIPs, ports, device grids, and multi-die topology; the timing database carries crossing, routing, and delay models. A cross-database engine keeps topology, resource IDs, routing endpoints, and timing coherent as resources are added or replaced, then regenerates the databases for Vivado implementation and validation.
